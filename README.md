# 🚀 SkillTrack 360°

## Skill-to-Livelihood Outcome Intelligence & Intervention System

> **Don't just track outcomes — understand why they happen, predict risks early, intervene intelligently, and measure the impact.**

SkillTrack 360° is a consent-based, longitudinal skilling outcome tracking and impact-measurement platform designed to track a trainee's journey beyond training and certification.

It connects:

**Training → Certification → Employment / Self-Employment / Apprenticeship → Verification → Retention → Wage Progression → Skill Gap → Intervention → Impact**

# 📌 Problem Statement

Skilling programmes commonly capture enrolment, attendance, assessment, certification and training completion. However, information about what happens **after training** may remain incomplete.

Important outcomes such as employment, self-employment, apprenticeship, job retention, wage progression, relevance of training, skill utilization, reasons for non-placement and reasons for attrition may not be consistently tracked.

### Key Challenges

- Trainees may change phone numbers or locations.
- Employers may not report consistently.
- Different programmes may use different identifiers.
- Employment definitions may vary.
- Follow-ups may be incomplete.
- Employment information may remain unverified.
- Long-term outcomes may not be available in one continuous record.

This makes it difficult to understand the **real impact of skilling programmes** and identify what needs to be improved.

## 2. 🔍 Outcome Gap Detector

The system distinguishes between:

```text
EMPLOYED
SELF-EMPLOYED
APPRENTICE
UNEMPLOYED
UNKNOWN
UNVERIFIED
```

> **Unknown outcome does not automatically mean unemployed.**

The system can identify unreachable trainees, pending verification, overdue follow-ups, conflicting information and incomplete records.

*Any numbers used in demonstrations are sample data only.*

## 3. 🚨 Early Outcome Risk Detector

The Early Outcome Risk Detector identifies trainees who may be at risk of poor placement or retention outcomes.

### Example Signals

- Assessment performance
- Practical performance
- Required skill gaps
- Interview readiness
- Certification status
- Engagement signals
- Apprenticeship participation
- Job requirement mismatch
- Previous placement attempts
- Trainee feedback

### Example Risk Profile

```text

Risk Score: 78 / 100
Risk Level: HIGH

Contributing Factors:
• Practical Score: 42%
• Assessment Score: 48%
• React Skill: Weak
• Git Skill: Weak
• Interview Readiness: Low

→ Industry Project
```

The key idea is:

> **Detect → Explain → Intervene → Measure**

## 4. ❓ Why Not Placed Engine

Instead of showing only:

> "38% trainees are unplaced"

the system attempts to explain **why**.

Possible reasons include:

- Technical skill gap
- Practical skill gap
- Interview readiness
- Location mismatch
- Salary expectation mismatch
- Lack of employer opportunities
- Certification issue
- Communication / interview issue
- Insufficient practical exposure

This changes the decision-making process from:

```text

      ↓
WHY did it happen?
      ↓
WHAT should be done?
```

# 🚨 Risk Engine — Prototype

For the prototype, the Early Outcome Risk Detector can use an **explainable rule-based scoring system**.

| Signal | Example Condition | Risk Contribution |
|---|---|---:|
| Assessment | < 50% | +20 |
| Practical | < 50% | +25 |
| Required Skill | Missing | +20 |
| Interview Readiness | Low | +15 |
| Engagement Signal | Low | +10 |

### Risk Levels

```text
0 – 30     LOW
31 – 60    MEDIUM
61 – 100   HIGH
```

These values are **prototype assumptions** and are not official government thresholds.

The system should explain not only the risk score but also the major contributing factors.

# 🔗 Trainee Identity & Duplicate Resolution

Longitudinal tracking can become difficult when:

- Phone number changes
- Location changes
- Employer changes
- Multiple records are created
- A trainee returns to the system later

### Identity Model

```text
User Account
      ↓
Authenticated User
      ↓
Trainee Record
      ↓
Outcome Passport
```

The system should distinguish between a login account and the underlying trainee record.

### Matching Flow

```text
Exact / Verified Match
        ↓
Link Existing Record

Possible Match
        ↓
Manual / Assisted Verification

No Match
        ↓
Create New Record
```

Uncertain records should not be automatically merged.

# 🔐 Privacy & Responsible Data Use

SkillTrack 360° is designed around privacy-conscious outcome tracking.

Key principles include:

- Consent-based records
- Minimum necessary data collection
- Role-based access
- Controlled data visibility
- Verification status
- Secure authentication
- Responsible use of predictive systems

The platform does not assume access to government databases unless an authorized integration is provided.

The Outcome Passport is not an Aadhaar replacement or identity document.
